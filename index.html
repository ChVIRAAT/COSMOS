08 · Small bodies

Asteroids, comets & falling stars

The leftovers of planet formation. Some carry the organic chemistry of the early Solar System; all of them carry clues about how worlds are built.

Asteroid belt

Between Mars and Jupiter

Most asteroids orbit 2.2–3.2 AU from the Sun. Despite the crowded look in films, the belt is mostly empty space — the largest object, Ceres, holds about a quarter of its mass.

Meteor shower

Meteoroid → meteor → meteorite

A meteoroid is a small rock in space. When it burns in the atmosphere it becomes a meteor. If a piece reaches the ground, it is a meteorite. Showers occur when Earth crosses a comet’s debris trail.

Kuiper Belt & Oort Cloud

The deep freeze

The Kuiper Belt holds icy bodies from 30 to 50 AU; the hypothetical Oort Cloud may extend 100,000 AU, supplying long-period comets. Neither has been directly surveyed in full.

About COSMOS

A museum of the universe, open to everyone

COSMOS is an independent educational explorer. Every measurement shown is drawn from publicly available NASA, ESA, ISRO, JPL and Smithsonian material. Where a value is an estimate or a range, it is labelled as such. Hypotheses and theoretical concepts — dark energy, Hawking radiation, the Oort Cloud’s full extent — are marked so they are never confused with confirmed observations.

The 3D scenes are real-time WebGL models, not videos or static images. Planets are rendered with procedural or public-domain texture maps; orbits use true period ratios with compressed distances for legibility. No scientific claims should be used for navigation or critical decisions.
